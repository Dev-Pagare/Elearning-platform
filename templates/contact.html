Frequently Asked Questions

How do I enroll in a course?
Browse courses, click "View Course", then click "Request Enrollment". After admin approval, you'll have full access.
Are the courses self-paced?
Yes! All courses are fully self-paced. Learn at your own schedule, from anywhere, on any device.
What payment methods are accepted?
We accept UPI, Credit/Debit Cards, Net Banking, and popular wallets like Paytm and PhonePe.
Will I get a certificate?
Yes! Upon completing a course, you will receive a certificate of completion that you can share on LinkedIn.
How long do I have access to a course?
Once enrolled, you have lifetime access to the course content including all future updates.
Is there a refund policy?
Yes, we offer a 7-day money-back guarantee if you're not satisfied with the course content.
